Stop the migration?

Their sites keep their existing passwords. Keygrain will forget those services on this device and, after sync, on your other devices. It will no longer generate, copy, or fill passwords for them. Services you already marked as rotated stay in Keygrain.

Warning: At least one service has a stored TOTP seed. Stored seeds are not derivable: removing the service permanently deletes its seed from Keygrain, and you may need to re-enroll two-factor authentication on that site.

You can import a removed service again later if you decide to rotate it.
